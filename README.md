# 🚀 Overview

Traditional career platforms mainly focus on job listings and applications.

**IntelliMatch AI** introduces an intelligence layer that analyzes:

- 📄 Resumes
- 🧠 Technical skills
- 🎓 Academic profiles
- 💼 Experience
- 🛠️ Projects
- 📜 Certifications
- 💻 Coding performance
- 🎤 Interview performance
- 🎯 Career goals
- 🏢 Job requirements
- 📍 Location preferences
- 💰 Salary preferences

The platform transforms this information into actionable intelligence:

```text
Resume Intelligence
        ↓
Skill Intelligence
        ↓
Job Intelligence
        ↓
Career Intelligence
        ↓
Interview Intelligence
        ↓
Recruitment Intelligence
        ↓
Placement Intelligence
