# 🚀 What is IntelliMatch AI?

**IntelliMatch AI** is an intelligent career, talent, recruitment, assessment, and placement intelligence platform designed to connect:

- 🎓 Students
- 👨‍💻 Job Seekers
- 🏫 Colleges
- 👨‍🏫 Placement Officers
- 🧑‍💼 Recruiters
- 🏢 Companies
- 🤖 AI Career Agents

The platform creates a continuously evolving **Career Intelligence Profile** for every candidate.

Instead of simply asking:

> "What jobs are available?"

IntelliMatch AI asks:

> "Which career is suitable for this person, what skills do they currently possess, what skills are missing, which opportunities fit them, how prepared are they, and what should they do next?"
